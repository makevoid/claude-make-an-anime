Location painting for an anime production. {{STYLE}}

A broken circular stone arena floating high above a sea of clouds at a burning sunset. Cracked pale flagstones, a ring of snapped pillars, chunks of rock hanging in the air around the rim. Sky split in two: burning orange and magenta sunset on the left, a dark blue-violet thunderstorm with distant lightning on the right. Wide establishing view, no people, no creatures. No text or symbols carved anywhere.
