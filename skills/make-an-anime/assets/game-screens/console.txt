Video game interface art asset for an original 2004 PC real-time strategy game, high-end pre-rendered look of that era. Not from any existing game: an original design.

The image is one wide bottom HUD console, seen straight on, flat, front view, no perspective. It runs edge to edge across the full width of the image and fills only the bottom 30 percent of the image. Everything above the console is one flat, even, pure magenta colour (#FF00FF), with no gradient, no shadow and no texture, so it can be keyed out.

The console: heavy sci-fi armour plating in dark gunmetal and brushed steel, with chamfered edges, rivets, small vents, thin cobalt-blue glowing trim lines and a few amber status lamps. Its top edge is not straight: it steps up into a raised armoured pod at the far left and a second, lower raised pod at the far right, joined by a lower middle section.

Recessed into the console, from left to right, five empty screens, each with a bevelled metal bezel and a flat, solid black inside (#000000), with nothing drawn in them:
1. in the raised left pod, a tall portrait screen, about one tenth of the image wide;
2. a square minimap screen next to it;
3. in the middle, one wide, low rectangular information screen, about a third of the image wide;
4. a narrow vertical slot beside it;
5. in the raised right pod, a command-card area: a grid of 4 columns by 3 rows of empty square button sockets, each socket flat solid black inside.

Crisp, clean, symmetrical bevels, sharp detail, readable at small size. No text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no icons, no logos, no buttons with drawings on them, no characters, no units, no map, no watermark.
